Services

Practical financial management for owners and management teams who want structure without the overhead of a full in-house finance function.

  • [01]Budgeting & forecasting

    Annual budgets and rolling forecasts built around how your business actually earns and spends.

  • [02]Cash flow management

    Short- and medium-term cash planning, working-capital review and early visibility of pressure points.

  • [03]Management reporting

    Concise monthly reporting packs and KPIs that show performance at a glance.

  • [04]Cost control

    Structured review of overheads and supplier spend to protect margins.

  • [05]Process design

    Clear workflows for approvals, month-end close and record-keeping that scale with the business.

  • [06]Part-time finance

    Flexible, ongoing support for companies that need senior financial oversight a few days a month.
